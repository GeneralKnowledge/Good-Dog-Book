# External influences (concepts)

**Status:** Living doc. Influences shape voice and pedagogy; **evidence** still comes from register sources (E2).

## *Easy Peasy Puppy Squeezy* — Steve Mann

Popular UK owner guide (force-free, basics-focused). Detailed map: [`research/influence-easy-peasy-puppy-squeezy.md`](../research/influence-easy-peasy-puppy-squeezy.md).

**Integrated concepts in *Good Dog*:**

- **Puppy optimism** — feel safe first, then teach (ch13).  
- **Three Ds** — distance, distraction, duration; one at a time (ch05).  
- **Mutually exclusive behaviours** — teach something incompatible with the problem (ch11).  
- **Cue → mark → pay** — teaching loop (ch03–04); parallel to EPPS, not exclusive to it.
