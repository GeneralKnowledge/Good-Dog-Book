# House style

## Language

- **British English** throughout.  
- **Second person** for instructions (“Hold the treat…”); **first person plural** sparingly for shared owner experience (“We’ve all…”).

## Terminology

- Introduce technical terms in plain English first; full definitions in back matter / glossary.  
- Use [`continuity/terminology.md`](../continuity/terminology.md) as the spelling and usage register.  
- “Marker word” or “yes word” before jargon “secondary reinforcer” unless glossary entry exists.

## Formatting (manuscript)

- Markdown chapters in `manuscript/` named `chNN-slug.md`.  
- Use `##` for major sections; `###` sparingly.  
- **Practice lab** sidebars: blockquote prefix `> **Practice lab.**`  
- **Evidence note** (maintainer-facing during draft): HTML comment `<!-- EVIDENCE: claim-id E2 verified 2026-... -->` — strip or convert to notes before print.

## Instructions

- Numbered steps for procedures.  
- Each procedure includes: **You need**, **Try this**, **Look for**, **If it’s not working**, **Stop if**.  
- Observable success criteria — no mind-reading the dog.

## Citations

- Chapter notes: `(Dogs Trust, n.d.)` or `(DEFRA, 2010)` per `research/evidence-policy.md`.  
- Do not footnote every sentence; footnote material welfare and contested claims.

## Final-audit grep targets (banned unless quoted critically)

- guaranteed, always works, cures, dominant, alpha, pack leader  
- IMDT approved, vet approved, clinically proven  
- stubborn, disobedient (as character explanation)

## Accessibility

- Plain language score target: readable by a tired owner on a phone.  
- Avoid walls of bullets; break with short paragraphs.
