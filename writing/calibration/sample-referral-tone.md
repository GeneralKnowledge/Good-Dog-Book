# Calibration — referral tone

If your dog has started growling over food, guarding the sofa, or snapping when touched, this book can still help you **manage safely** — but it cannot replace a qualified professional who can assess the whole picture.

Pain makes liars of the nicest dogs. Sudden behaviour change deserves a vet check before we blame training or “attitude.” That’s not drama; it’s sensible.

In the UK, anyone can call themselves a trainer. Look for transparent, reward-based methods and clear referral pathways when problems are serious. ABTC and IMDT publish **public** guidance on ethical practice; mentioning them is not the same as this book being endorsed by them.

Asking for help early is not failure. It’s the part of dog ownership that adults do when they love the dog more than the fantasy of being the hero in a montage.
