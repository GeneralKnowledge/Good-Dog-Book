# Further reading (optional)

**Status:** Reader-facing signpost (E3/E4). Not required to use this book. **No author or programme endorsement.**

This book is designed as a **cookbook**: bookmark the chapter you need. These sources add depth in a UK reward-based frame when you want another voice on the shelf.

## Welfare and method defaults

| Source | Why it might help |
| --- | --- |
| [DEFRA Code of Practice for the Welfare of Dogs](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/code-of-practice-for-the-welfare-of-dogs) | UK baseline: teach kindly, meet welfare needs |
| [Dogs Trust — dog advice](https://www.dogstrust.org.uk/dog-advice) | Practical articles aligned with our loose-lead, recall, visitor, and puppy beats |
| [RSPCA — dog behaviour](https://www.rspca.org.uk/adviceandwelfare/pets/dogs/behaviour) | Short sessions, welfare-first tone |

## Finding qualified help (UK)

| Source | Why it might help |
| --- | --- |
| [ABTC — find a clinical animal behaviourist or trainer](https://www.abtc.org.uk/) | Signpost to registered practitioners; market is not fully regulated (`claim-abtc-001-unregulated`) |
| IMDT / force-free ethics (public pages) | Referral culture when problems exceed kitchen-scale teaching (`claim-imdt-003-referral`) |
