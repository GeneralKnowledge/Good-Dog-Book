# ch01 — The deal you’re making with your dog

## Unique contribution
Frames training as a **fair contract** (clarity, kindness, realistic expectations) — not obedience theatre or dominance.

## Practical outcome
Reader can explain why reward-based training fits everyday life and what the book will/won’t promise.

## Prerequisites (reading order)
None (read #1).

## Sections
1. What “good dog” means in real kitchens and muddy parks  
2. What training can and can’t fix (management vs teaching)  
3. **Training the Human** — train the human first; owner mantra *“Make it easier — then ask again.”* (developed in ch04)  
4. **The loop and five checks** (names only — ch04, ch12, ch14)  
5. How to use this book: [`CHAPTER_MAP.md`](../CHAPTER_MAP.md) read order; **ch12 before live skills**; problem paths (scared on walks → ch12 first; recall worries → ch09 after ch07–ch12; stuck anywhere → five checks / ch14)
