# ch01 — The deal you’re making with your dog

## Unique contribution
Frames training as a **fair contract** (clarity, kindness, realistic expectations) — not obedience theatre or dominance. Sets expectation: this book will **train you** to stay patient and kind when progress wobbles (stealth self-help), not only to cue the dog.

## Practical outcome
Reader can explain why reward-based training fits everyday life and what the book will/won’t promise.

## Prerequisites (reading order)
None (read #1).

## Sections
1. What “good dog” means in real kitchens and muddy parks — casual tone; mantra callback  
2. What training can and can’t fix (train / manage / vet / behaviour help) + **what messes dogs up (usually us)**; **house → garden → outside** place ladder  
3. **Training the Human** — witty frame (*train the human first*); owner stress leaking to the dog; mantra *“Make it easier — then ask again.”* / *Good human.* (developed in ch04)  
4. **Train yourself / train the dog** preview — **leisure walk vs training walk** (no loose-lead mechanics; ch10 for lead teaching); Know / Do / Don't (full pattern from ch04)  
5. **Where the how-to lives** — pointer only to ch04, ch12, ch14; **no** teaching-loop or five-check names in ch01 prose  
6. How to use this book: read order; **ch12 before live skills**; three plain **problem-path bullets** (detail in ch14 / chapter map)
