# ch09 — Settle, calm, and time alone

## Prerequisites (reading order)
ch07, ch04–ch08 (read #9).

## Unique contribution
Invited settling (mat, rest spot) vs forced “calm down”; household realism; **time-alone prevention** (added 2026-10-22).

## Practical outcome
Reader can teach settle-on-mat and a rest spot with clear stop conditions, and build time alone gradually — recognising distress as a referral, not DIY.

## Sections
1. Mat as optional landing pad  
2. Calm around movement — honest expectations  
3. Rest spot and choice to leave  
4. Time alone — prevention steps, before-you-leave, never punish, ≤4 h, signs of distress, what doesn’t help, when to get help
