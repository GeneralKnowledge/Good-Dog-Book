# Good Dog Book

**Working title:** *Good Dog: Training the Human* — practical dog training that also helps owners stay patient and kind when progress wobbles (warm, witty, British English, reward-based, welfare-centred).

**Stage:** First manuscript draft complete (~19k words); **Phase 3 editorial diagnosis** complete — see [`editorial/2026-10-18-phase3-whole-manuscript-diagnosis.md`](editorial/2026-10-18-phase3-whole-manuscript-diagnosis.md). **Next:** targeted revision (Stage G) after author approval.

This repository is independent of [Good Dog](https://github.com/GeneralKnowledge/Good-Dog) (the app) and [Ides](https://github.com/GeneralKnowledge/Ides) (fiction workflow reference). Research provenance is pinned to [DogResearch](https://github.com/GeneralKnowledge/DogResearch) snapshots; see [`sources/SNAPSHOT.md`](sources/SNAPSHOT.md).

## What this book is not

- Not veterinary or clinical behaviour advice  
- Not IMDT/ABTC endorsed unless a documented professional review says so  
- Not a catalogue of Good Dog exercises
