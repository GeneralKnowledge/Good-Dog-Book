# Scope and boundaries

**Status:** Living document (Stage B). **G1 approved** — see [`research/foundation-gates.md`](../research/foundation-gates.md).

## Feasibility in one volume

**Assessment:** The practical promise in `proposition.md` is achievable in **one book**. *(Original estimate ~70–90k words; author decision 2026-10-22: length lands naturally — second draft ≈40k words. See `research/decision-register.md`.)* Achievable if specialist clinical topics stay on **referral paths** rather than full treatment manuals.

## Primary audience (recommended)

Ordinary UK-oriented dog owners who want a **coherent method** for everyday life — not competitors, not professional trainers certifying others.

Secondary readers (experienced owners, new puppy guardians, rescue adopters) are served by **differentiated sections**, not separate books in this project phase.

## Out of scope (boundary only or bibliography)

| Topic | Book treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Clinical aggression/reactivity treatment plans | Referral + what owners can do meanwhile (management) |
| Separation-related distress as DIY cure | Prevention tips only; clinical cases → professional |
| Competition obedience, sport, working dog specialisms | Not covered |
| Breed-by-breed encyclopaedia | Not covered |
| Medication, diagnosis, veterinary behaviour medicine | Vet-only; book points to vet |
| Multi-dog household protocols | Flagged gap in DogResearch; brief mention only until researched |
| Guaranteed outcomes (“fixed in 30 days”) | Prohibited |

## Training the Human / owner mantra

The subtitle is **humour and wit** (owners as the trainee in the joke). The mantra and **Train yourself** blocks teach **owner habit change** — especially catching frustration before it becomes harsher training. Stealth self-help, not therapy copy. Neither is clinical advice, a guarantee, or a substitute for vet or behaviourist assessment.

## Good Dog app content

- **Do not** structure the book as ~28 exercise chapters.  
- Exercises may inspire **sidebars** or **practice labs** with explicit adaptation notes.  
- Material with audit status “substantial revision” or “qualified review” is **not book-ready** until resolved (see `research/good-dog-gap-reconciliation.md`).
